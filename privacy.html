Privacy Policy — Nights Publisher

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Nights Publisher ("the App") is a private desktop tool used by one person, the owner of the YouTube channel Nights of Mythology ("the Owner"), to upload and schedule videos on YouTube channels the Owner owns. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to data handled by Google.

What data the App accesses

  • youtube.upload — to upload videos to the Owner's channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl — to read the Owner's own channel ID (to confirm the right channel), set thumbnails and upload caption tracks on the Owner's own videos.

The App only reads the Owner's own channel basic information (channel ID and title). It does not read, collect or store data from any other YouTube user, comments, subscribers or analytics.

How data is stored

  • The OAuth token is stored only on the Owner's computer, in a local file, and is never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • Videos, thumbnails, captions, titles and descriptions are created by the Owner and sent only to YouTube.
  • The App has no server, no database, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies.

Sharing

The App does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting the local token file also removes the App's access. After revocation the App stops all API use and keeps no YouTube data beyond what the Owner created.
